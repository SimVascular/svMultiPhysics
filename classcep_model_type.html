Vector<double> cepModelType::D

Additive conductivities along the fiber, sheet, and sheet-normal directions.

\(D[0]=D_f\), optional \(D[1]=D_s\), and optional \(D[2]=D_n\). The diffusion tensor is

\[ D = D_\mathrm{iso}\, I + \sum_i D[i]\, f_i \otimes f_i. \]
