9/**
10 * @brief Land-Niederer active stress model.
11 *
12 * This class implements the phenomenological Land-Niederer active stress model
13 * [1], which represents the cross-bridge cycling and calcium-troponin binding
14 * kinetics in a single set of ODEs. The cross-bridges are modeled as a three-state
15 * system, with transitions between unbound (U), pre-power stroke (W), and post-power
16 * stroke (S) states. The blocked state (B) represents the tropomyosin blocking
17 * the actin binding sites, and the unblocked state (U) repesents the availability
18 * of these sites for cross-bridge binding. The unblocking of the tropomyosin is
19 * regulated by the calcium-troponin binding state (CaTRPN) and the fiber stretch.
20 * The distortion of the cross-bridges are also represented for the pre-power stroke
21 * and post-power stroke states. The active tension is computed as a function of the
22 * cross-bridge states and their distortions, scaled by a reference tension Tref.
23 * This is given as
24 * @f[
25 * \Tact = L(\bar\lambda) \frac{Tref}{r_s} \left[ (1 + \zeta_S) X_S + \zeta_W X_W \right]\;,
26 * @f]
27 * with @f$\bar\lambda=\min(1.2,\fiberstretch)@f$ and
28 * @f$L(x)=\max(0,1+\beta_0(x+\min(0.87,x)-1.87))@f$, implemented by @ref length_dependence_factor.
29 * @f$X_S@f$ and @f$X_W@f$ are the fractions of cross-bridges in the post-power
30 * stroke and pre-power stroke states, respectively. @f$\zeta_S@f$ and @f$\zeta_W@f$
31 * are the distortions, and @f$r_s@f$ is the steady-state duty ratio of the
32 * cross-bridges.
33 *
34 * Calcium is expected in uM, after the scaling by @c Calcium_scaling_factor
35 * set in the @c <Active_stress> XML element (see @ref activestress-inputs).
36 * Since electrophysiology models usually provide calcium in mM, inputs coupled
37 * to one need @c Calcium_scaling_factor set to 1000; it defaults to 1.0, and a
38 * warning is printed if it is not given. Setting
39 * @c Disable_force_strain_rate_feedback in the same element removes the fiber
40 * stretch rate term from the @f$\zeta_W@f$ and @f$\zeta_S@f$ distortion ODEs.
41 *
42 * **References**:
43 * 1. [Land, Niederer (2017)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.yjmcc.2017.03.008)
44 */
