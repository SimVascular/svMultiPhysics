17/// @brief Update the vectors that the linear solver uses for the tangent
18/// contribution of a resistance or coupled boundary condition, so that they
19/// follow the deformed geometry under a follower pressure load.
20///
21/// Computes the integral over the face of N_a * n for each node a, where N_a
22/// is the shape function of the node and n is the normal (see Brown et al.
23/// 2024, Eq. 56), and stores it in the linear solver face
24/// com_mod.lhs.face[lBc.lsPtr] twice:
25/// - as unit_pressure_load, in the intermediate configuration of the
26/// generalized-alpha method, where the residual of the follower pressure
27/// load is assembled;
28/// - as flowrate_derivative, in the configuration at the end of the time
29/// step: it is the derivative of the flow rate with respect to the nodal
30/// velocities.
31///
32/// The flow rate of a struct equation coupled to svZeroD or is integrated on
33/// the deformed face, so it also depends on the nodal displacements. Its
34/// derivative with respect to them, with the velocity held fixed, is added to
35/// flowrate_derivative multiplied by beta*dt/gamma: the ratio between the
36/// changes of displacement and velocity that an increment of the unknown
37/// produces. This is currently not supported for ustruct or svOneD (struct
38/// cannot currently be coupled to svOneD).
39///
40/// For a coupled boundary condition with a cap, the contribution of the cap
41/// is appended to flowrate_derivative. The linear solver reads the two vectors
42/// in add_bc_mul().
43///
44/// @param[in,out] com_mod The simulation data. Only
45/// com_mod.lhs.face[lBc.lsPtr] is modified.
46/// @param[in] lBc The boundary condition.
47/// @param[in] lFa The face of the boundary condition.
48/// @param[in] solutions The solution states, from which the displacement and
49/// the velocity are read.
50void fsi_ls_upd(ComMod& com_mod, const bcType& lBc, const faceType& lFa, const SolutionStates& solutions);
