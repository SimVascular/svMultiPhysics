std::pair< std::vector< PetscInt >, std::vector< PetscInt > > PetscLinearAlgebra::PetscImpl::coupled_neumann_dofs ( const ComMod & com_mod) const

Find the degrees of freedom where the two vectors of the coupled Neumann faces can be nonzero.

They are the degrees of freedom of the nodes of the Neumann faces that init_dir_and_coupneu_bc() included, for the pressure load, and of the nodes of their flow rate derivative, for the flow rate derivative. They do not depend on the values of the two vectors. Only the nodes owned by this process are considered.

Parameters
[in]com_modThe solver data, whose dof and lhs are read.
Returns
The O2 indices of the degrees of freedom of the pressure load (first) and of the flow rate derivative (second), sorted and without repetitions.
